Released: January 21st, 2025
Genres: Synthpop, Experimental, Avant-Garde, Indie Pop, Alternative Rock
Run Time: 22:28

Glasswork Images is Royal Fish's 2026 winter EP, announced at Royal Fishmas 2025. It released at midnight on January 21st, 2025 on all platforms. The album has 6 tracks, with the first and last songs following the naming scheme of their previous winter EP, High Street Lows. The EP's total runtime is 22 minutes and 28 seconds.

Album Cover

The album cover is a combination of several photographs and photo manipulation techniques. The eyes of the band members are cut out, put through a threshold effect, and mismatched. The background is multiple photos overlayed on a blue abstract background. The band's name and the EP title are feature on the bottom right in light blue in a mixed italics style.

The photographs of the band's eyes were taken by the band's official photographer, Lorenzo Mazzeo, after WCWM's Battle of the Bands. The overlayed background photographs are night photography taken by Emily.
