Royal Fishmas (Concert)

Royal Fishmas is a yearly concert in which the band plays holiday music, sells merch, and offers fun holiday activities.

History

The first Royal Fishmas was on December 6th, 2024, the last day of classes. It was held in a lecture hall, Andrews 101.
