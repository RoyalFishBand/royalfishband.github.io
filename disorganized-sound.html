Disorganized Sound is the band's ambitious collaborative album, created during the Spring semester of 2026 and released on May 24th, 2026. This album was created because of the absence of Major Ellie.

Album Cover

The album cover was created on a standard white posterboard cut into a square, with a section marked off for the title, which was later added digitally. Everyone who is featured on the album was invited to draw something on this posterboard. Many of the drawings were made by Emily and Dan, who each drew a picture for every song they were involved in creating.

History

This album was first conceptualized by Emily upon hearing of Major Ellie's absence for the purposes of study abroad. She wanted to continue work on the band, but didn't want to play public-facing live shows without their drummer. She was inspired to make a collaborative album based on William Bibeau's recent project, which also featured Royal Fish, Orgy Not Included. Dan and Emily posted an Instagram reel inviting other groups on campus to collaborate with them on a special album. Below is a list of the groups that agreed and recorded on the album.
